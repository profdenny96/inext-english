🧩 How do I build the sentence?

Opinion
Expression + complete idea

I really feel that + we need more time.

I really feel that we need more time.

The way I see it, + the project needs more examples.

Suggestion
I suggest we + base verb + complement
I suggest we finish the project today.
How about if + subject + verb…?
How about if we meet after class?
Agreement
Agreement expression + optional reason
That’s a good idea. Let’s do that.
That’s fine by me.
Polite disagreement
Soft disagreement + reason / alternative
I’m not sure that I agree, actually. I have another class.
That’s a good point, but what about tomorrow?

🎧 Listening · Page 42

Antes do áudio, faça o aluno prever: qual opção parece uma opinião, sugestão ou discordância?

Complete with the correct option

  1. The way I see it / The way I see things / I really feel that working part-time could increase productivity.
  2. What about / How about if / I suggest we think about trying a flexible schedule for the next month and evaluating the results?
  3. That’s OK by me / That’s fine by me / I am not sure that the proposed solution will meet our long-term goals.
  4. I suggest we focus on giving employees the freedom to manage their hours more independently.
  5. That’s a good point / That’s a good idea / I am not sure that I agree, actually, but perhaps we should consider the team’s ideas before deciding.

Listening strategy

Não tente traduzir tudo. Procure primeiro a função da expressão: opinião? sugestão? acordo? discordância?

🔧 Grammar toolbox · Verb to be + qualities

Present
Subject + am / is / are + quality
I am confident.
She is responsible.
They are creative.
Past
Subject + was / were + quality
She was very responsible.
They were reliable.
Negative
She isn’t punctual.
They weren’t organized.
They aren’t confident or creative.
Question
Is she reliable?
Were they punctual?
