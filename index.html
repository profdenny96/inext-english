03 · Grammar toolbox

🧩 Monte sua frase

Escolha os blocos; observe as fórmulas e veja como muda do presente para o passado.

Verb to be

Present: I am · She is · They are
Past: I was · She was · They were

She was a doctor. → Was she a doctor?

Pergunta: BE + sujeito; negativa: BE + not.

Simple Present / Simple Past

I want → She wants → She wanted

Does she want to work? / Did she work?

Com do / does / did, o verbo fica na forma base: WANT, WORK.

Sentence Builder · Construção guiada

Frequency expressions

After BE: She was always on time.

Before other verbs: She often worked at night.

always · usually · often · sometimes · rarely · never. “Normally” e “occasionally” também aparecem no livro.
